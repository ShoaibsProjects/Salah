    PREPARING FOR WORSHIP

    A clear beginning.
    Care before prayer.

    Preparation makes space for worship: purification, suitable covering, the prayer time and a clean place facing the Qiblah. Each essential can be learned calmly, one step at a time.

    01

    Time & intention

    The prayer’s time has entered. The intention is held sincerely in the heart for Allah.

    02

    Purification & covering

    Purification, clean clothing and suitable covering are attended to with care, without excess.

    03

    Direction & space

    The worshipper faces the Qiblah as able, in a suitable clean place, with a boundary in front.

    Visit the original publication

    Editorial approach

    Educational explanations use a calm third-person voice, concise paragraphs and clear source links. Quotations are distinguished from summaries. Personal duʿā and transmitted recitations retain their own wording.

    Learning with a teacher

    This companion has not received independent scholarly review. It offers a sourced beginner path, without claiming endorsement or providing personal fatwas. A qualified teacher can help with pronunciation, religious questions and individual circumstances.

    Respecting the publication

    The prayer guide is © Salafi Publications 2021, all rights reserved. Its PDF and publisher illustrations are not bundled here. Further-reading links open the original publication; the app’s explanations are original summaries.
