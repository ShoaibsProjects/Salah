    BEFORE I STAND BEFORE ALLAH

    A little preparation.
    Then I begin.

    I take a moment to prepare, without turning preparation into a burden. I do what I can with care, then place my trust in Allah.

    01

    I make room

    I look for a clean place, face the Qiblah as I am able, and let the day wait for a few moments.

    02

    I prepare

    I attend to purification and suitable covering, taking each step gently and without excess.

    03

    I turn with intention

    I know which prayer I am offering. What I carry in my heart is for Allah; no spoken formula is needed here.

    How I shaped these notes

    I wrote original, brief explanations to help a learner follow one coherent beginner path. The main reading reference is the prayer description by Shaykh Al-Albānī, presented for English readers by Abu Khadeejah Abd al-Wāhid Alam. I do not reproduce or rehost their PDF, illustrations, or pages, and this companion is not endorsed by its author or publisher.

    My intention here

    I hope these words make it easier to learn and return to prayer. I am not a scholar, and this companion has not received independent scholarly review. It is a learning aid, not a fatwa or a judgment on anyone’s worship. For personal rulings, ask a qualified teacher you trust; for recitation, learn with someone who can listen and help.

    Respecting the source

    The PDF is © Salafi Publications 2021, all rights reserved. Its website explains the conditions for sharing and reuse. I link to the original rather than copy it here. Page links open the relevant place in that source.
