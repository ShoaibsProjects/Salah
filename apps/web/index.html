    Our approach

    The learning notes are original summaries, not a reproduction, authorized edition or endorsement by the author or publisher. Short recitations are identified by their source. Printed page references are used throughout; the PDF page is three higher.

    Salafi scholarship includes differences

    The guide follows Al-Albānī. We compared relevant rulings with Ibn Bāz’s official website. Differences on the sutrah, Al-Fātiḥah behind an imam, hands after bowing and entering prostration are labeled where they matter.

    01 / YOUR CHOICES

    A place.
    A day. A method.

    Search the offline city directory, use an available location fix or enter coordinates. The embedded map suggests a timezone; after you confirm it, the engine fills today's date.

    Search larger towns and cities without sending your query to a server.

    A compass tells direction, not location. This starts with one energy-conscious browser request to fill latitude and longitude. If no fix is available, you may choose a more precise request, which can use more power. This page does not send coordinates to Salah or call an online lookup. Your device may use GPS, Wi-Fi, cellular, or a recent fix; some sources need internet. The result is an estimate, not exact. You can enter coordinates yourself.

    Location permission is not requested unless you choose the button.

    Open the list to choose from the embedded time zones. Type the first letters to jump to a name. Coordinates can suggest one automatically.

    Confirm the timezone first. The Rust engine fills today in that zone. Pick or type another date here to switch to manual date.

    Choose the calculation convention used by your community. The Sun-angle choices give estimated Fajr and Isha beginnings; there is no universal selection here.

    After the Sun passes its highest point, the engine checks how much an object's shadow has grown beyond its shadow at noon. Standard uses one object-height of extra shadow; Hanafi uses two. Their calculated times can differ. Follow the practice your mosque or trusted scholar recommends.

    Example: 1 October 2026, published MWL angle parameters and Hanafi Asr. This is an explicit research configuration, not a recommended local default.
