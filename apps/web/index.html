Choose the calculation convention used by your community. The Sun-angle choices give estimated Fajr and Isha beginnings; there is no universal selection here.

After the Sun passes its highest point, the engine checks how much an object's shadow has grown beyond its shadow at noon. Standard uses one object-height of extra shadow; Hanafi uses two. Their calculated times can differ. Follow the practice your mosque or trusted scholar recommends.
